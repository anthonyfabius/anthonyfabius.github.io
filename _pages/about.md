I'm a graduate student in Computer Science at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, where I work with Prof. Chen Wang on theoretical complexity and approximation bounds for correlation clustering. My broader research interests are in sub-linear, streaming, and dynamic graph algorithms for high-performance, memory-constrained systems.

Outside of research I've interned as a software engineer at Google and Everpure, and I co-lead a couple of open-source systems projects — see the [projects page](/projects/) for details.
